Imagine living forty thousand years ago, when one freezing night could mean death.
A young boy watches his family struggle through a brutal winter.
The wind is freezing, their animal skins are barely keeping them warm, and the temperature keeps dropping.
But then, his mother picks up something strange.
A tiny needle made from animal bone.
She threads it with animal sinew and begins stitching pieces of animal hide together.
Slowly, she creates clothing that fits more tightly around their bodies.
The next morning, the family steps outside.
The freezing wind is still blowing.
But something has changed.
Their new clothing helps trap body heat and keeps the cold air out.
And here's the surprising part!
Archaeologists have actually discovered ancient bone needles, showing how early humans developed clever sewing techniques.
Sometimes, surviving the Ice Age didn't require a powerful weapon or incredible strength.
It required something much smaller.
A tiny needle... and a brilliant human idea.
